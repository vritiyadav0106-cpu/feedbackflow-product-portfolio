Featured project — FeedbackFlow

End-to-end product case study: feedback inbox + RICE prioritization for early-stage teams.

Role Product lead (discovery → MVP)
Timeline 2 weeks (MVP)
Status Shipped

Problem

Early-stage product teams receive feedback across Slack, email, support, and sales — but lack a simple system to capture, theme, and prioritize that feedback into a buildable backlog.

Solution

FeedbackFlow centralizes feedback in an inbox, lets teams promote themes to feature ideas, and ranks the backlog using RICE scoring — without enterprise tool complexity.

What I owned

  • Problem discovery, personas, and competitive positioning
  • PRD, user stories, and MoSCoW scope decisions
  • North star metric (TTPD) and activation funnel design
  • Functional prototype to validate the core workflow (not a production build)

Key decisions

  • Cut auth & integrations — validate workflow before infrastructure
  • RICE over ICE — effort dimension reduces “shiny feature” bias
  • Prototype over slides — prove the loop works in under 10 minutes

Metrics

  • North star: Time to prioritization decision (TTPD)
  • Activation: First feedback logged → first feature scored
  • Adoption: % of backlog items with complete RICE scores
