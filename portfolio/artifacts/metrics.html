FeedbackFlow — Metrics plan

North star & funnel · Vriti Yadav

North star

Time to prioritization decision (TTPD) — hours from receiving a batch of feedback to having a ranked top-three backlog.

Target for the primary persona: reduce weekly prioritization time from ~4 hours on spreadsheets to under 1 hour using a single inbox + RICE workflow.

Activation

MetricDefinition
First feedback loggedUser adds first inbox item in session one
First feature scoredUser completes RICE on at least one backlog item

Leading indicators

  • Share of feedback promoted to features (inbox → backlog loop)
  • Share of backlog items with complete RICE inputs
  • Time from promote to first scored feature

How I’d measure in production

Instrument the three core events (feedback created, feature promoted, RICE saved), weekly cohort review with PM users, and qualitative checks in stakeholder reviews — can they defend the top three without reopening spreadsheets?
