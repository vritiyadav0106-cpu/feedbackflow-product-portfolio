FeedbackFlow — Case study narrative

Vriti Yadav · Product case study

Problem

Early-stage teams collect feedback across support, sales, Slack, and interviews — but rarely turn it into a ranked, defensible backlog. Priorities become whoever spoke loudest last.

Users

I focused on a first PM at a ~25-person B2B startup who needs credibility fast without enterprise tooling, and a student product lead who needs structured prioritization for portfolio and internship conversations.

Solution

FeedbackFlow is a browser MVP with three screens: an inbox for tagged feedback, a backlog with RICE scoring and auto-sort, and a dashboard for counts and top priority. Core loop: capture → promote → score → decide.

Scope decisions

  • No login or integrations in v1 — validate the workflow before infrastructure
  • RICE over gut feel — explicit tradeoffs for stakeholder conversations
  • Working prototype over slides — demo the loop in under ten minutes

Prioritization example

Using RICE, CSV export scored higher than SSO for the primary persona (reach and effort aligned with early-stage needs), which informed what I would ship first in a real roadmap.

Outcome

The MVP proves the feedback-to-backlog loop. Next steps I’d pursue: CSV import, linking multiple feedback items to one theme, then team collaboration once retention on the core loop is validated.
