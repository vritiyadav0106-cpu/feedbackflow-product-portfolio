# Interview-Ready Checklist

Use this before every application or interview. Check boxes honestly.

---

## Portfolio setup

- [ ] Ran `./start.sh` or `Start Portfolio.command` successfully
- [ ] Clicked through Dashboard, Inbox, Backlog in FeedbackFlow
- [ ] Personalized `PERSONALIZE.md` and updated portfolio contact info
- [ ] Deployed online (GitHub Pages or Netlify) — optional but recommended
- [ ] Portfolio URL on resume and LinkedIn

---

## Knowledge (can you explain without notes?)

- [ ] Problem statement in 2 sentences
- [ ] Priya's Monday morning scenario
- [ ] RICE formula + one worked example
- [ ] 3 things you cut from MVP and why
- [ ] North star metric + 2 supporting metrics
- [ ] v1.1 vs v2 roadmap items
- [ ] One "what I'd do differently" answer

---

## Demo (practice once on video)

- [ ] 60-second pitch recorded and reviewed
- [ ] 5-minute live demo under time
- [ ] Added feedback live in Inbox during demo
- [ ] Changed RICE score and explained re-sort
- [ ] Closed with roadmap next step
